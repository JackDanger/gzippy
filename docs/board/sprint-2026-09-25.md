Three loss classes, three investigations, three evidence packets. This branch

## 1. pigz L9/T4 — we are 1.38× slower (the biggest wall loss)

**Mechanism (agent-16, confirmed by local sampling):** NOT the scheduler, NOT
the matchfinder (T1 is 3.3× faster than pigz-9 there). The T>1 route runs
`level::params_parallel(9)` ≡ `params_parallel(11)` — the near-optimal parser
at L11 knobs — *plus* the unscoped ×4 depth multiplier (100 → 400) from the
else-branch at `level.rs:539`. Per-byte cost ≈ 4× the Lazy2 parse everything
else was graded against; 4 threads ÷ 4.3× cost ≈ 0.93 — matching the measured
1.07× aggregate scaling exactly. Local first-party confirmation (M1, 106 MB):
our T4 wall is 1.17× our own T1 (pigz's is 0.25×).

**Fix lever:** a middle parse for L9 at T>1 — per-strategy depth scoping
(400→100) and/or bounded optimization passes — measured against the size
ledger (the floor: reverting to the regular parser regressed text +23,413 B).
Probes: (1) config-cost matrix on one 1.8 MB chunk through
`encode_deflate_splice_chunk_to_sink` variants (parallel vs params(9) vs
near-opt@100 vs near-opt@100+passes1 vs Lazy2@2400) — discriminates causes
within one afternoon; (2) parallel-efficiency via `compress_parallel` +
`GZIPPY_DEBUG=1` phase atomics (predicted to EXONERATE the scheduler); (3)
DONE — the routing asymmetry is pinned as a unit test in
`src/compress/deflate/level.rs` (`t1_vs_parallel_l897_routing_asymmetry_is_deterministic`).

## 2. T1 Ir-vs-port (L1 + L6 cells; L2/L9 are framing-tax only)

**Mechanism (agent-17):** `level_uses_ldx` excludes L1/L3/L6/L7 deliberately —
each refusal carries a banked size cell. At L2/L4/L5/L8/L9 the shipped engine
IS the port (margins 1.013–1.017 = framing+inventory tax ~0.3–0.5 Ir/B). At
**L6** the gap is mostly the depth knob (128,65,g8 vs the port's 35,65 —
d9418505 moved it to the zlib-arm knobs, with 2–4 flip receipts) plus ~15 Ir/B
legacy-driver tax. At **L1** the gap is the legacy per-position machinery,
partly purchased (hash3, bucket2, lazy-peek, REACH dense inserts — each with a
size-cell receipt); the 2.04× noise cell is the diagnostic: 59.7 Ir/B of bare
per-position scan code with zero match amortization. Absolute Ir is still
37–53% below pre-restructure main.

**Probes:** P0 sheet re-grade on CI (existing). P1 L6 depth isolation via
`--features ladder-tune` + `GZIPPY_LADDER=lazy:35:65` (and a middle rung
`lazy:64:65`) under cachegrind + the size gate (tie-guard, won_cells,
ladder_is_monotone_t1). P2 L1 engine isolation: extend `examples/ir_runner.rs`
with an `ldx_gzip` arm and grade ours vs ours-as-ldx at L1, then check the
retention cells (pigz −1 text, armexe binary class). P3 framing-tax split
(ldx vs ldx_gzip vs ours at L2/L9). P4 anatomy counters on the noise cell.

## 3. T(N) byte-parity

**Mechanism (agent-18):** divergence is intentional (params_parallel +
Generous headers + per-T chunk grid coupling) — size-impacting by design; the
trunk passes roundtrip at every T. The fix is a design decision: unify
engines/params/grid per level. Trade-off: promoting the parallel knobs to T1
changes T1 bytes (board-adjudicated); retiring them reopens the T4-L8/9 size
wins (+23,413 B text if fully reverted).

**Landed on this branch:** the contract corrections (stale "byte-identical
regardless of num_threads" claims in `pipelined.rs` / `pure_parallel_encoder.rs`),
and the red-first parity gates (`tests/thread_byte_parity.rs`) — three tests,
currently failing as designed, that turn green the day unification lands and
then enforce byte-parity at any N with roundtrip protection.
